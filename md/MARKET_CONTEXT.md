# Market Context

## Global Collectibles Market

The collectibles market includes assets such as:

- coins
- stamps
- art
- antiques
- memorabilia

These markets function as both:

- hobby markets
- alternative investment markets

---

## Market Inefficiencies

The industry suffers from:

- limited data transparency
- fragmented information
- inconsistent pricing

---

## Key Market Participants

### Collectors
Buy items for hobby or investment.

### Dealers
Buy and resell collectibles professionally.

### Auction Houses
Facilitate high-value transactions.

### Reference Databases
Provide catalogs and pricing guides.

---
