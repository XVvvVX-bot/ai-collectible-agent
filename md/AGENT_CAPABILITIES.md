# Agent Capabilities

The AI Collectibles Intelligence Agent performs the following tasks.

---

## 1. Item Identification

Inputs:

- images
- textual descriptions
- known attributes

Outputs:

- probable item type
- catalog identification
- variant classification

---

## 2. Price Intelligence

The agent analyzes:

- historical auction records
- marketplace listings
- comparable sales

Outputs include:

- estimated market value
- price range
- comparable items

---

## 3. Market Trend Analysis

The agent analyzes price history to detect:

- rising markets
- declining demand
- cyclical trends

---

## 4. Rarity Estimation

Rarity indicators may include:

- population reports
- auction frequency
- catalog rarity ratings

---

## 5. Research Assistance

The agent can answer questions such as:

- historical context of collectibles
- grading standards
- identifying variations

---

## 6. Decision Support

Example use cases:

- determining fair purchase price
- evaluating auction opportunities
- assessing resale potential
